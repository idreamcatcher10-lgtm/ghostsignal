# GhostSignal product steering — externally prepared

- Our product is an offline, human-carried QR pager, not a mesh messenger.
- Keep users aware of what happens on their device and when they share messages physically.
- Design for low connectivity, unreliable cameras and users in a hurry.
- Prefer small, reversible increments. UI must work on 320px mobile and desktop.
- Never claim tested functions, Kiro usage or emergency reliability without evidence.
- Never introduce account requirements, an API key, cloud storage or tracking in the MVP.
